# Next TODO Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: October 19, 2026 / 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日

## English

Next TODO does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- No account or sign-in.
- No network connection. The app does not send anything to the developer or to any third party.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- Your tasks and preferences are stored only on your Mac, in a container shared between the app and its widgets. Deleting the app removes them.

Contact: https://github.com/Trade-Offf/NextUp
