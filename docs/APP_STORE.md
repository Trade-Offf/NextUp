## English (U.S.) — 主语言

- 名称：Next TODO
- 副标题：Simple to-dos, always in view
- 推广文本：A private to-do list for your Mac. Add tasks from the menu bar and see what's next right on your desktop. No account, no cloud.
- 关键词：todo,tasks,checklist,widget,menu bar,reminder,planner,outline,productivity,focus,local,private
- 描述：

```
Next TODO is a small, quiet to-do list for your Mac. Write things down, see what comes next, and get on with your day.

WRITE FAST
Type a task and press Return. Paste a whole outline and it becomes a task list with sub-tasks, up to three levels deep. Right-click to rename, set a due date, or delete. Undo and redo work the way you expect.

SEE WHAT'S NEXT
Click the menu bar icon to add a task from anywhere, with the three most important tasks right below the field.

TWO DESKTOP WIDGETS
"What's Left" shows how many tasks remain: overdue, today, later and unscheduled. "Next TODO" lists your upcoming tasks by date, and you can tick one off straight from the desktop.

MADE FOR THE MAC
Choose your own keyboard shortcuts. Pick small, medium or large text for your display. Light and dark appearance, VoiceOver and keyboard navigation are supported.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no network access. Your tasks stay on your Mac.

Available in English, English (Australia), Français, 日本語, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.
```

## 简体中文

- 名称：Next TODO
- 副标题：接下来做什么，一眼就看见
- 推广文本：Mac 上的私人待办。在菜单栏随手记，在桌面上看接下来要做的事。不登录，不上云。
- 关键词：待办,任务,清单,小组件,菜单栏,提醒,计划,大纲,效率,专注,本地,隐私
- 描述：

```
Next TODO 是 Mac 上一份小而安静的待办清单。记下来，看清接下来要做什么，然后继续你的一天。

记得快
输入任务，按回车。粘贴一整段大纲，会变成带子任务的清单，最多三层。右键可以改标题、设置截止时间或删除。支持撤销和重做。

看得到接下来
点菜单栏图标，随时随地添加任务。输入框下面是最值得看的三件事。

两块桌面小组件
「还剩多少」显示剩余任务数：逾期、今天、以后和未排期。「接下来」按日期列出即将到来的任务，可以直接在桌面上点圆圈完成。

为 Mac 而做
快捷键可以自己设置。文字大小有小、中、大三档，适应不同的显示器。支持浅色和深色外观、VoiceOver 和键盘操作。

隐私优先
不用注册，不用登录，没有广告，没有统计，也不联网。任务只保存在你的 Mac 上。

支持 English、English (Australia)、Français、日本語、简体中文和繁體中文。
```
